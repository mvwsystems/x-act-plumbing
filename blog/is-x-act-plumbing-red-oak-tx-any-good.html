How to verify a plumber's credentials in Texas

In Texas, plumbers are regulated at the state level, so you never have to take a license claim on faith. Before you hire anyone in Red Oak — X-Act included — do these three things:

  1. Confirm the license. Ask for the Master or Responsible Master Plumber license number and look it up on the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners lookup. A legitimate company will hand this over without hesitation.
  2. Ask about insurance. Liability insurance protects your home if something goes wrong during the work. Request proof, not just a verbal yes.
  3. Get the scope and price in writing. Reputable shops describe the work and the cost before they start, so there are no surprises when the invoice arrives.
Pro tip: A "Master Plumber" and a "Journeyman" are not the same license. For anything involving your gas line, water heater, or repiping, you want the job performed or supervised by a Master Plumber. Ask which license the person at your house actually holds.

Why local matters in Red Oak, TX
