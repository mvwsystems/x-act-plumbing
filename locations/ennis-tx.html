Bluebonnet City Plumbing

Historic Homes.
Modern Plumbing.

Ennis — home of the state's official Bluebonnet Trails and the National Polka Festival — is Ellis County's second-largest city, and its housing tells a long story: pier-and-beam homes near the historic downtown, mid-century neighborhoods, and brand-new slab subdivisions on the edges. Each era fails differently, and X-Act Plumbing has worked on all of them.

In the older blocks, that means original cast iron drains reaching the end of their service life and galvanized supply lines choking flow — we replace both, including full repipes to PEX. In the newer sections, it's slab leak detection and builder-grade water heaters aging out. Wherever your Ennis home falls on that timeline, the plumber who shows up is a licensed Texas Master Plumber, straight down I-45 from Red Oak.
