Built on Clay.
We Know What That Means.

Glenn Heights straddles the Dallas–Ellis county line along I-35E and has been one of the fastest-growing cities in the southern metro — subdivision after subdivision of slab-on-grade homes built on North Texas Blackland Prairie clay. That expansive clay moves with every wet-dry cycle, and slab foundations and the plumbing inside them feel it. X-Act Plumbing, based minutes away in Red Oak, handles slab leak detection and repair here week in and week out.

The city's building waves also mean whole neighborhoods hit the same milestones together: builder-grade water heaters aging out 10–15 years after the subdivision went in, and original fixtures due for replacement. Whether your home is in a brand-new phase or one of the established neighborhoods, you get a licensed master plumber — not a franchise crew — at your door.

Glenn Heights, TX FAQ

Common Questions

Do you serve all of Glenn Heights, TX?

Yes — X-Act Plumbing serves every Glenn Heights neighborhood, on both the Dallas County and Ellis County sides of the city. We're based minutes away in Red Oak, so emergency and same-day response times are short.

Who handles slab leaks in Glenn Heights?

We do — slab leak detection and repair is one of our core services. Glenn Heights' slab-on-grade homes sit on expansive Blackland Prairie clay, which stresses foundations and under-slab plumbing. We pinpoint the leak electronically before any concrete is cut.

My builder-grade water heater is 12 years old. Should I replace it?

Most builder-grade tank water heaters last 10–15 years, and in Glenn Heights entire subdivisions often hit that window together. If yours is showing rust, noise, or lukewarm water, replacing it on your schedule beats an emergency swap after it fails. We install tank and tankless units.
